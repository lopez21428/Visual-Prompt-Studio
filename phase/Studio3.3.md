Tradition and Transformation: Beyond the Ideal
Name: Jahaziel Lopez
Historical tradition: Greek Idealism
Modern social value: Identity and Self-Expression
Artistic style: Fauvism

Studio Prompt
Create a promotional landing page for a fictional art exhibition titled “Tradition and Transformation: Beyond the Ideal.” Combine Greek Idealism with the modern value of Identity and Self-Expression, using Fauvism as the main artistic style.

Show an original group of Greek-inspired marble sculptures representing people with different body types, facial features, ages, and cultural identities. Place them among classical columns, challenging the idea that there is only one perfect human appearance. Give each figure equal visual importance.

Use vivid, non-naturalistic Fauvist colors, expressive brushwork, and simplified forms. Surround the pale marble figures with saturated blue, orange, magenta, and green shapes. Contrast geometric columns and rectangular text panels with organic shapes around the figures.

Use curved contour lines to define the figures and diagonal directional lines to guide the viewer from the exhibition title toward the central artwork and then down to the event details. Create strong value contrast between the bright marble sculptures and a deep blue background, making the central group the focal point.

Include clearly readable exhibition text: “Many identities. No single ideal.” Add fictional event details: “November 14–December 12, 2026 | Unity Arts Gallery,” and a button labeled “Explore the Exhibition.” Present the result as one polished vertical landing-page image.

Vocabulary Breakdown
Contemporary Art
To me, contemporary art uses many different materials, shapes, and colors to express politics and the world around us today.

Pop Art
To me, Pop Art takes images from mainstream culture, like comic books, celebrities, and popular products, and gives them a creative spin.

Directional Lines
Directional lines are strokes that guide the viewer’s attention through shapes and movement. In my image, they lead the viewer toward the sculptures in the center.

Value Contrast
Value contrast is the difference between light and dark. In my design, the light surfaces of the statues contrast with the dark blue background, making them stand out.

Focal Point
The focal point is the area that draws the viewer’s attention. In my design, it is the group of sculptures and their differences. Each has distinct qualities, and no single figure is presented as the ideal, giving each one equal value.

Fauvism — Additional Term
To me, Fauvism depicts natural things using unrealistic colors.
