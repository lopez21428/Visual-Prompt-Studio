Prompt 1: Hope for a Successful Hunt
Historical theme: Art connected to survival, ritual, and belief
Historical period: Paleolithic
Inspiration artwork: Cave paintings at Pech Merle
Medium: Cave painting using charcoal and earth pigments on stone

Create a Paleolithic cave painting featuring a large bison as the main focus. Use charcoal black, red ochre, and earthy brown pigments on a rough stone wall. Give the bison bold contour lines and a recognizable body shape, using the natural curves of the rock to suggest volume. Surround it with smaller animal outlines and stenciled handprints. Use repetition to create rhythm and make the central bison larger to establish emphasis. Avoid modern objects, lettering, and realistic landscape backgrounds.

Imagine this painting as part of a hunter-gatherer community’s ritual expressing hope for a successful hunt. The bison represents animals that provided food and materials essential for survival, while the handprints suggest community participation. This purpose is a possible interpretation of prehistoric cave art, rather than a proven explanation of Pech Merle.

Prompt 2: Royal Protection in the Afterlife
Historical theme: Art and the afterlife
Historical period: Ancient Egypt, New Kingdom, 18th Dynasty
Inspiration artwork: Funerary Mask of Tutankhamun
Medium: Gold funerary mask with colored glass and stone inlays

Create an ancient Egyptian royal funerary mask inspired by Tutankhamun’s mask. Show an idealized face with a calm expression, outlined eyes, a ceremonial false beard, and a striped blue-and-gold nemes headdress. Include a cobra and vulture at the forehead and a broad decorative collar. Use a frontal composition with symmetrical balance. Emphasize the face through its central placement, polished gold surface, and contrast with the deep blue inlays. Repeat the headdress stripes and collar patterns to create rhythm.

The mask is intended to cover and protect a deceased pharaoh’s mummy and help preserve his identity in the afterlife. Its idealized features express the Egyptian belief in eternal existence, while its royal symbols connect kingship with divine protection. Precious gold and elaborate craftsmanship reflect the wealth and specialized labor available to the royal court. Present the mask against a simple dark background, without modern clothing, lettering, or objects.
