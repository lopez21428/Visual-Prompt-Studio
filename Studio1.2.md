# Studio 1.2 immersive album cover challenge
## genre 
synthwave /retro 1980s

## Complete prompt 
Create a square album cover image in a synthwave /retro 1980s style showing a man doing parkour across rooftops of a futuristic city. Capture him mid-air, jumping from rooftop to rooftop.  Use neon blue and purple lights against deep shadows to create a strong value contrast and an energetic nighttime mood. Create depth by placing the parkour athlete large. In the foreground, with overlapping buildings that become smaller and fade into the distant background. Showtime in motion through the athlete's mid-air pose. Flowing clothing and glowing speed lines trailing behind him as he jumps. Create Unity by repeating the same neon blue, purple, and pink color palette throughout. The composition add variety with a warm, bright orange circular sun behind the athlete to break the pattern and create visual interest. Use asymmetrical balance by placing the oversized athlete slightly off-center while the glowing sun and city buildings balance the opposite side. Make the athlete the largest element to create clear visual hierarchy.

## vocabulary breakdown 
value: How dark or light the colors and area of an image appear 
space: The area around, between, and inside objects that helps create depth 
unity: When all the visual elements work together and make the design feel connected 
Balance: How visual weight is arranged throughout the image so it feels stable 
Rhythm: The repetition of colors, lines, or shapes that creates movement throughout a design
